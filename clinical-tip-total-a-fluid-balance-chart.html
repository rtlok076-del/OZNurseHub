Clinical tip 06 · Fluid balance

Total a fluid balance chart

Your fluid balance chart says plus two litres… but does it really? Add every column, check it twice, and watch the trend — not just the total.

Watch how to total a chart cleanly, then practise on four fictional patients with the free fluid balance simulator.

Total a fluid balance chart

In − Out = Balance. Check it twice. Watch the trend.

Take a moment to watch this short clinical learning video, then reflect on what stood out to you.

  • Add every intake column: oral, IV, NG or enteral, and anything else. Then add those totals together.
  • Do the same for every output column: urine, vomit, drains, NG aspirate and stool.
  • Balance is total in minus total out. More in is positive. More out is negative.
  • Check it twice: add across the rows and down the columns. Both should match.
  • Watch the trend, not just the total. One column can be falling all afternoon while the balance looks fine.
  • If something looks off, tell your RN or team leader early, and follow your local policy.

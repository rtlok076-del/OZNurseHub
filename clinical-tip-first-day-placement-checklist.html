Clinical tip 04 · Placement

Your first day on placement checklist

A calm first shift starts the night before. A few minutes of preparation means you can focus on learning, not on what you forgot.

Watch the night-before checklist, then reflect on what you want to get out of your first shift.

Watch & reflect

Your first day on placement checklist

First day tomorrow? Pack this tonight.

Take a moment to watch this short clinical learning video, then reflect on what stood out to you.

  • Pack the night before: student ID, fob watch, a black pen, a small notebook, water and a snack.
  • Check your uniform, following your uni’s rules, and wear comfortable closed shoes.
  • Know where and when: where to go, who to report to and what time to arrive. Aim to be a little early.
  • Introduce yourself to your supervising RN with your name, year level and one or two goals for the shift.
  • Keep patient details out of your notes and follow local guidance on what you can write down.
  • If you’re unsure, ask. That’s what placement is for.

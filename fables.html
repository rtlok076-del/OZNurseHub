Story-based learning

Fables that help nursing lessons stick

These short stories are designed to make practical nursing ideas easier to remember. They’re not replacements for the clinical content — they’re the memory hooks that go with it.

Featured fable

The Magpie and the Too-Busy Shift

Story: A magpie tries to collect every shiny thing on the ward and ends up dropping the most important one.

Reveal: Busy is not the same as effective. When everything feels urgent, prioritisation is what keeps the shift moving.

Lesson: Write the list, sort the list, then act on the list — especially when the noise goes up.

How to read the fables

  • Read the story first
  • Look for the reveal
  • Apply the lesson to a real ward situation

Planned themes

  • prioritisation on a busy shift
  • speaking up when something feels off
  • handover and communication
  • confidence when you don’t know everything
  • staying calm when the ward gets loud

Sample format

Story: A small animal gets overwhelmed by shiny tasks.

Reveal: Doing everything at once feels productive, but it often delays the task that actually matters.

Lesson: Prioritise, write it down, and work the list in the right order.

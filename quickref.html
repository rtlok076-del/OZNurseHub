Nursing Quick Reference Guide for Australian Students & New Grads

This quick reference page provides the essential clinical values every Australian nursing student and new graduate nurse needs to know during placement and clinical practice. The normal adult vital signs table covers temperature, heart rate, blood pressure, respiratory rate, SpO₂ oxygen saturation, blood glucose level (BGL) in Australian mmol/L units, and Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS). These ranges are based on international consensus guidelines and should always be verified against your specific facility's clinical policies and the Australian Commission on Safety and Quality in Health Care standards. The nursing abbreviations list includes common terms used in Australian hospital documentation including PRN, QID, TDS, IDC, NGT, NBM, PCA, and BSL/BGL — always verify approved abbreviations with your health service.

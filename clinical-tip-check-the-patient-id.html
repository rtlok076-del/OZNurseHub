Clinical tip 03 · Patient safety

Check the patient ID every time

Positive patient identification means confirming you have the right patient, using approved identifiers, before you give any care.

Watch how a similar name can lead to the wrong patient, then reflect on how you check ID on every shift.

Watch & reflect

Check the patient ID every time

Same name. Different patient.

Take a moment to watch this short clinical learning video, then reflect on what stood out to you.

  • Use at least three approved identifiers, such as full name, date of birth and MRN/URN.
  • Ask open questions, such as “Can you tell me your name and date of birth?”, rather than yes/no questions like “Are you Sam?”
  • Match the wristband to the chart, the order and the label.
  • Check every time: before medicines, bloods, procedures, transfusions and transfers, and at handover.
  • If anything doesn’t match, stop and clarify before you go on.

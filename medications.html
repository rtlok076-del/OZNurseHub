A few things worth knowing

Australian names are used here, including paracetamol, salbutamol, adrenaline, noradrenaline and glyceryl trinitrate. Search also recognises several overseas names. Specialist or non-routine entries from the original study list are clearly labelled.

These cards do not cover every interaction, contraindication or monitoring requirement. Formulation and route can change a medicine's uses and risks. Do not start, stop or change medicines using this page. No dosage recommendations are provided.

Make sense of the abbreviations

AF: atrial fibrillation

DVT / PE: deep vein thrombosis / pulmonary embolism

DKA: diabetic ketoacidosis

COPD: chronic obstructive pulmonary disease

OCD: obsessive-compulsive disorder

MDD: major depressive disorder

XR: extended release

NSAID: non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug

SSRI: selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor

SNRI: serotonin-noradrenaline reuptake inhibitor

NDRI: noradrenaline-dopamine reuptake inhibitor

STEMI: ST-elevation myocardial infarction

QT prolongation: delayed electrical recovery of the heart, increasing arrhythmia risk

PDE5 inhibitors: medicines such as sildenafil; unsafe with nitrates

ACE: angiotensin-converting enzyme

NMDA: N-methyl-D-aspartate receptor

SGLT2: sodium-glucose co-transporter 2

GLP-1: glucagon-like peptide-1

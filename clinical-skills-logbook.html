Questions for reflection

Think these through or discuss them with your educator. No answers are collected.

  1. What happened, and what was your role?
  2. What went well?
  3. What was difficult, and what would you do differently?
  4. What did you learn? How does it connect to theory or an NMBA standard?
  5. What will you practise or ask about next time?

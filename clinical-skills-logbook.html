What the levels mean

These are for your own record. They're not a uni assessment scale, and your uni's skills record comes first.

Keep it de-identified

  • Describe the skill, not the person. "Wound dressing on a lower leg" is enough.
  • Leave out patient names and initials, bed or room numbers, dates of birth, record numbers and unusual details that could identify someone.
  • Use your supervisor's initials only, and only if they're happy for you to.
  • Don't log anything you weren't allowed to do. If you're unsure whether a skill is in your scope, ask before, not after.
  • If an entry looks like it includes a name with a title, a record number, a date of birth, a bed number, a phone number or an address, the logbook won't save it until you take it out.

    Step 4 / PDF summary and backup

    Print it, sign it, back it up.

    Your PDF summary has a cover page, a table of every skill with dates and levels, your reflections if you choose to add them, and a sign-off box for your facilitator. It's made on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

    Your details for the cover

    Only you see these. They're saved on this device and printed on the cover page.

    What to include

    Get your PDF

    • Computer: choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.
    • iPhone or iPad: tap Print, then Share, then Save to Files.
    • Android: choose "Save as PDF" at the top of the print screen.

    Backup and move devices

    A backup file holds your whole logbook. Keep it somewhere private. Restore it here on a new phone or computer. Entries that look like they include patient details are left out when you restore.

    Start again

    Delete your whole logbook from this device, including custom skills and cover details. Download a backup first if you want to keep it.

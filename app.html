One tap away

Add OzNurseHub to your device.

Free to install. Open it from your home screen or apps list, with the same content as the website.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Open the Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Enable Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.

Android

Use the Install button above when available, or choose Install app or Add to Home screen from your browser menu.

Desktop

In Chrome or Edge, use the install icon in the address bar or the browser’s app menu. On a Mac, Safari also offers Add to Dock.

Your skills record stays in this browser or app on this device. Installation does not create an account or sync between devices. Use the logbook backup before switching browsers or apps. Open the app online first to save its core pages for offline access; external links, downloads and online features may still need internet.

Learning support, not clinical direction. Follow current local policies and supervision requirements.

    Enrolled nurses

    The enrolled nurse pathway, briefly.

    • Registration works the same way. Enrolled nurses (ENs) qualify with a Diploma of Nursing and apply to the Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia (NMBA) through Ahpra's graduate application, so the Ahpra steps in the timeline and checklist apply to you too.
    • Different standards. ENs work to the NMBA Enrolled nurse standards for practice: 10 standards in three domains. ENs work under the direct or indirect supervision of an RN. If you're building STAR answers for an EN role, link them to the EN standards instead.
    • Grad and transition programs. Some programs take newly qualified ENs. For example, WA's GradConnect includes ENs, and Tasmania runs a transition to practice program for ENs. Check your state's page and local employers.
    • Medicines. What an EN can do with medicines depends on their education. Read the NMBA fact sheet on enrolled nurses and medicines administration, and check your employer's policy.
    • EN to RN. Many universities give credit for a Diploma of Nursing towards a Bachelor of Nursing. Ask your uni what credit you'd get. Many ENs keep working as ENs while they study.
